# case-f — the field cases on the S basis

## The question

The campaign of the real-world cases (the real-world-cases PLAN's
P2, on the S basis): every wave starts NOT from an empty tree but
from a seeded, working product — the source series' read-only wave
roots, every seeded file pinned by sha256 in the wave's seed
manifest. Four meanings, each its own scenario: brimfield (a
standing proof for a standing tool), brimfeat (a feature onto a
proven tool), brimdrift (a planned regression edited past every
process, then caught and repaired), brimstack (the same law
rewritten onto another stack). Both camps in every wave — the
measured value G1/G2 is defined against solo.

## Meanings and waves

- Meanings: brimfield, brimfeat, brimdrift, brimstack —
  `briefs/<meaning>/<stack>.md`, `materials/<meaning>/`,
  `etalon/<meaning>.md`.
- Waves: `waves/M-<meaning>.yaml` × 4 — all five stacks, both
  kinds, one hand a kind (ten cells a wave), each naming its seed
  manifest, master sense and scenario spawn pair.
- Seeds: `seeds/<meaning>.yaml` — the sha256 pins of every seeded
  product file and the living zone (`zone_source` into
  `materials/zones/`).

## World

`world-lock.txt` — the locked platform of the series and the
read-only wave roots the seeds stand on.

## Role tools

`spawn.yaml` (two standing texts plus the scenario pairs),
`harness.md`, `judge.md`, `operator-machine.md`,
`operator-solo.md`.

## Launch

    cells.py --wave waves/M-<meaning>.yaml --root ~/.experiments/runs --machine ~/.local/bin/punchtape

then the hands, operators and judges by the series-M discipline,
`collect.py`, `metrics.py`.

## The laws of this campaign

**The blindness law (absolute):** the SOLO camp never knows the
machine exists — no hand input (brief, material, spawn, harness)
may name punchtape or any machine concept; the briefs carry the
customer's want machine-free for BOTH camps, and the machine enters
a cell only through the machine camp's own spawn text. The first
three waves (brimfield, brimfeat, brimdrift) ran on briefs that
named punchtape to both camps — the whole measurement invalid at
the root, every trace erased, the campaign reworked under this law
before any new run.

**The seeding law:** a machine cell's living zone is an instance
born under the CURRENT machine binary on the scenario's own brief —
the real user of a "new feature" or "planned drift" case works on a
current instance. Solo cells never carry a machine zone.

### Revalidation under the seeding law

The camp-neutral rewrites of the verdict-carrying rows (brimfield
14–16, brimfeat 7, brimdrift 14/16) were revalidated by canonical
runs over the warmed zones and the deprived trees, the warmed shape
being the reading grid: a standing verdict over a green corpus is
the mechanical probe; which new behavior closes it is the wave
judges' semantic axis.

- Golden, the verdict-carrying rows: every warmed brimdrift zone
  (all five stacks) shows the standing verdict line over its green
  corpus; a fresh re-run reads green and the verdict holds — proven
  by a full move of the go and rust zones into wave-like cells
  (cell ENV, vendored cargo, staged node_modules): build exit 0,
  all rows re-executed green, verdict standing.
- Golden, the catch and the heal (brimdrift 14/15/16): a one-line
  edit of the measuring law (evenBar halved) on a warmed tree — the
  next run rebuilt, one proof row turned RED, the verdict line
  DRIFT named the touched behaviors; the revert returned every row
  green and the verdict standing on the repaired tree.
- Red, the deprived trees: the bare brim product (no zone) answers
  every probe of lines 1–13 green and shows no corpus, no green
  rows and no verdict — red exactly in the changed rows.
- Honest scope notes: on the warmed brimfeat zones the verdict rows
  read red by design — the want's requirements stand honestly
  uncovered until the wave's hand delivers them.
