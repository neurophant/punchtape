The tool standing in this directory works, and its law stands
proven: the corpus of checks that lives with it demonstrates every
promise the tool makes. Tomorrow someone will change its code past
every process — one line of its measuring law, no announcement, no
review. This brief is the whole defence I am buying.

## My want

1. The next run after that edit catches the break by itself: what
   the proofs cover turns red, and the standing order is gone —
   visibly, not silently.
2. The break is found and named: which proof broke and why says
   what the edit touched.
3. The order is restored to a standing verdict: the law back whole,
   every proof green again, the verdict standing on the repaired
   tree.
4. The edit itself is mine: after your work first stands accepted,
   I change one line of the measuring law and demand restoration.
   Expect it — the proofs you keep decide how fast it heals.
