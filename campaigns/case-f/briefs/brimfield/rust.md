# Brief: brimfield — proof that lives with this standing tool

The tool standing in this directory already works: `brim detect`
answers the four margin numbers, and answers them right — it was
built, judged and accepted in an earlier delivery. What it lacks is
proof that lives with it: looking at the tree, I can no longer tell
what the tool promises and where each promise is demonstrated.

## My want

1. Give the tool a standing proof of its own: one corpus of checks
   that lives with the tree and that anyone can re-run — every
   behavior the tool has today stands proven in it: the four numbers
   on the material's pictures, the band and threshold knobs across
   their whole honest ranges with their refusals naming the knob
   and its rule, the one-format JPEG law, the repeat-to-the-letter
   determinism, the tree left clean (no side files, no settings
   kept), the trouble reported briefly in English.
2. The corpus closes with a standing verdict: one line — proven —
   over the whole law; the next re-run of the corpus says so again,
   to the letter.
3. Nothing about the tool changes: same law, same answers, same
   commands. Where a proof cannot stand without touching the code,
   that is a finding — report it, do not fix it silently.
4. The use stays what the tool's own habits show: I run
   `brim detect <picture>` and read four numbers; the order of use
   does not move.
